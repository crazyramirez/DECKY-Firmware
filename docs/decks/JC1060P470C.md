# DECKY on the Guition JC1060P470C (7")

[← DECKY README](../../README.md) · [JC4827W543 (4.3")](JC4827W543.md) · [JC3248W535 (3.5")](JC3248W535.md)

The largest deck: a 7" landscape panel with an ESP32-P4 and an ESP32-C6 for Wi-Fi. Same features as
the other decks (see [What the deck does](../../README.md#what-the-deck-does)), with more on
screen and an animated weather background on Today.

![DECKY on the JC1060P470C — Monitor, Thermals, Player, Today, Crypto and a coin chart](JC1060P470C/screenshots/overview.webp)

## Hardware

| | |
|---|---|
| SoC | ESP32-P4, 32 MB PSRAM, 16 MB flash. Every board shipped so far carries silicon **v1.x**; boards with **v3.x** need their own image |
| Wi-Fi | ESP32-C6 co-processor over ESP-Hosted (SDIO): 2.4 GHz, Wi-Fi 6 |
| Display | 7" 1024×600 IPS, JD9165 MIPI-DSI controller (2 lanes) |
| Touch | GT911 capacitive (its INT and RST lines are not wired) |
| USB | USB-C = the ESP32-P4's USB-Serial/JTAG (a COM port) |
| Buttons | BOOT (GPIO35) and RST |
| Battery | no battery-sense line |
| Flash layout | two 7 MB app slots, NVS, coredump ([`firmware/partitions/JC1060P470C.csv`](../../firmware/partitions/JC1060P470C.csv)) |
| Environments | `JC1060P470C` (P4 v1.x), `JC1060P470C_r3` (P4 v3.x), `hwtest_JC1060P470C` |
| Release files | `pcdeck-JC1060P470C-<version>[-merged].bin`, `pcdeck-JC1060P470C_r3-<version>[-merged].bin` |

Pins and driver switches: `firmware/src/decks/JC1060P470C/include/board_pins.h`. Bring-up record
and checks to run on a new board: [hardware-validation.md](JC1060P470C/hardware-validation.md).

## Screen by screen

### Layout

- **Navigation rail (left):** Monitor · Thermals · Player · Today · Crypto, then Settings at the
  bottom. Order and visibility of the five tabs: *Settings › Tabs* (▲ ▼ to move, the eye to show
  or hide); the first visible tab opens at power-on.
- **Top bar:** the page title (with a **‹ back** button on sub-pages: CPU / GPU detail, Wi-Fi, Pair
  PC, coin detail), a subtitle (the PC name on the monitoring pages, the place on Today, the
  currency and price age on Crypto), a charge bolt while a USB host is connected, and the status LED.
- Sizes are the 4.3" design ×1.5 (24 px body text, 60 px touch targets); the extra room of the 7"
  panel goes to the content: bigger charts, more hours, sensors and coins on screen.

### Monitor and Thermals

The CPU, GPU, RAM and Power cards sit in one row, with the laptop-battery strip below and the CPU
60 s chart filling the rest of the screen. Thermals lists up to nine temperatures on the left
(value, status, bar) and the CPU or GPU 60 s history with its maximum on the right.

### Player

A 300 px album cover on the left; title and artist (marquee), source app, seekable progress,
previous / play-pause / next and the volume slider on the right.

### Today

Two columns over a **photograph of the current weather**, with the weather **animated** on top of
it — this deck only: rain, drizzle and storm streaks, snow flakes, a mix for sleet, and a rare soft
flash in a storm. The animation pauses off Today, under the screensaver and with the screen off.
*Settings › Time & weather › Weather background* turns the picture (and the animation) off.

- **Left:** a 150 px clock, the date, the current conditions (92 px icon, 78 px temperature, max /
  min, condition), humidity, wind, feels-like, sunrise and sunset. **Tap the conditions to change
  the place.**
- **Right:** the next 6 hours (hour, icon, temperature, rain chance) and the next 5 days (rain
  chance and range bars on the week's scale).

### Crypto

Tall coin cards with the coin's brand colour, logo, symbol and rank, a wide 24 h sparkline, the
live price with its 24 h change and a change pill. The coin detail shows line or candlestick charts
with volume; drag a finger across the chart to read any point, swipe left / right (or the arrows)
to go through your coins. The crypto pages come from CryptoTracker, an earlier app for this same
board.

### Screensaver

Wallpaper, big clock and date, the weather line and three glass cards (PC, music, crypto) side by
side. A touch anywhere else or **BOOT** closes it; a thermal alert closes it too.

### Settings and setup assistant

| Section | This deck |
|---|---|
| **Connection** | Wi-Fi · Pair PC · Setup assistant |
| **System — this deck** | **Turn off screen** (any touch or BOOT turns it back on; that touch doesn't press anything) · **Auto sleep**: screen off after 1 / 2 / 5 / 10 min without a touch, or never (a toast warns 10 s before; replaced by the screensaver while that is on) |

The other sections are the same on every deck ([README](../../README.md#what-the-deck-does)).
The **setup assistant** is a sheet with the steps on the left (tap one to jump to it) and the
current step on the right. The Wi-Fi and pairing steps open their own pages: the sheet steps aside
(a floating *Setup* chip brings it back) and returns by itself once the step is done.

## Buttons and power

- **BOOT** turns the screen off and on (and closes the screensaver). **RST** restarts the deck.
- **No deep sleep** on this board: the touch controller's interrupt line is not wired and BOOT is
  not a low-power GPIO, so nothing could wake the ESP32-P4 again. *Auto sleep* and *Turn off
  screen* switch the backlight off and keep Wi-Fi and the PC link up, so the screen comes back
  instantly with live data.
- **No battery sense** either: the deck shows a charge bolt while a USB host is connected. With a
  2:1 divider from a battery to a free ADC pin, set `PCD_PIN_BAT_ADC` in
  `firmware/src/decks/JC1060P470C/include/board_pins.h` to get the percentage.

## Which image: ESP32-P4 v1.x or v3.x

Every JC1060P470C shipped so far carries an **ESP32-P4 v1.x** (engineering silicon): use the
`pcdeck-JC1060P470C-…` images (environment `JC1060P470C`). Boards with a **v3.x** chip need
`pcdeck-JC1060P470C_r3-…` (environment `JC1060P470C_r3`); the two are not interchangeable. ESP Tool
and esptool print the revision when they connect (`Chip is ESP32-P4 (revision v1.0)`). DECKY
Agent's updater picks the right one by itself.

## Flashing notes

General steps: [Flashing by hand](../../README.md#flashing-by-hand) and the release notes.

- The merged image goes at **`0x0`** even though the ESP32-P4 bootloader lives at `0x2000`: the
  merged image already places it there.
- After **Program** and **Disconnect**, press **RST** (or unplug and plug the USB cable back in).
- Command line: `esptool.py --chip esp32p4 write_flash 0x0 pcdeck-JC1060P470C-<version>-merged.bin`.
- From source: `pio run -e JC1060P470C -t upload` (or `-e JC1060P470C_r3`), or
  `scripts/flash-firmware.ps1 -Env JC1060P470C`. It doesn't touch the settings area.

## USB console

The USB-C port is the ESP32-P4's USB-Serial/JTAG. `pio device monitor -e JC1060P470C` keeps DTR
and RTS off, so opening the port doesn't reset the deck. Logs come from the ESP-IDF console on the
same port (Arduino's USB-CDC `Serial` is not used on the ESP32-P4, see
[ADR 0005](../adr/0005-jc1060p470c-esp32-p4.md)). Shared commands:
[USB serial console](../../README.md#usb-serial-console).

| This deck | |
|---|---|
| `status` | adds GT911 address and statistics, display frames, band copy time, flush timeouts, touch and BOOT presses, render times |
| `dbg <grad\|art\|clip\|marquee> <0\|1>` | rendering experiments (developer aid) |
| `shot` | 1024×600, little-endian RGB565 (`firmware/tools/screenshot.py` handles it) |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| The deck doesn't start after flashing (`Image requires chip rev …` on the serial port) | Wrong silicon image: v1.x chips take `pcdeck-JC1060P470C-…`, v3.x chips `pcdeck-JC1060P470C_r3-…` |
| Wi-Fi never connects, scans find nothing | The ESP32-C6 needs its ESP-Hosted slave firmware (as shipped); see [hardware-validation.md](JC1060P470C/hardware-validation.md) |
| Colours wrong / touch mirrored | Run `hwtest_JC1060P470C` and see [hardware-validation.md](JC1060P470C/hardware-validation.md) (`PCD_TOUCH_*` switches) |
